# Roblox places: what a single-player web platform can borrow (research, 2026-10-19)

For the platform team and game makers. The owner asked: «изучи роблокс плейсы — там можно найти интересное для разработки».
The question here is **which Roblox systems keep players for weeks, which of them survive without a server, and what
our platform would need as blocks**. It is not "port a Roblox hit": CrazyGames already has the ports (§4.0).

**How sure is this.** The numbers come from third-party trackers (RoWatcher, GGAID via studiokrew, levelupplay, RoMonitor
quoted by press), Wikipedia and search snippets. Trackers disagree, often by 2× and sometimes by 10×, and many guide
sites are SEO copies. Where sources conflict, both figures are given. Monetization figures per game are mostly
unpublished: the Robux prices below are from fan wikis and guides. Re-check a number before you bet on it.
`[S]` = primary or official source, `[3rd]` = press or tracker, `[fan]` = fan wiki or guide, `[U]` = unverified or
conflicting.

## 0. In eight lines

1. **Roblox hits are mobile F2P loops remixed into a shared 3D space** (Naavik: "Roblox's biggest successes almost always follow this remix model"; Grow a Garden ≈ FarmVille/Hay Day). The *loops* are old and portable. The *space* (seeing other players, showing off) is what we can't copy.
2. **The viral layer spikes and collapses.** Grow a Garden: 22.3M concurrent players (Aug 2025) → 31.2K on 2026-10-18 (−99.9%). Steal a Brainrot: 25.8M (Oct 2025) → ≈ 97–215K. Steal an Egg: launched 2026-07-25, 14.3M on 2026-09-19. The evergreens (Brookhaven, Blox Fruits, Adopt Me!, MM2) hold 0.2–0.5M for years. Peaks come from **scheduled live events**, the floor comes from **weekly updates**.
3. **The retention engine is the same in nearly every 2025–26 hit:** a **clock** (shop restocks every 5 min, weather, events at fixed times) × **variance** (eggs/rolls with rarity tiers, mutations that multiply value ×2…×125) × **a collection** (index, bestiary, "1 in X") × **an income that keeps going** (income per second, offline growth) × **a reset** (rebirth).
4. **Most of that is single-player logic.** In Grow a Garden the restock and the weather are server-wide, but they are only a function of the clock, so a date/time seed gives every web player the same shop and the same storm. That claim is *true*, so we can say it.
5. **What does not survive without a server:** trading economies with real value (MM2, Adopt Me!, Blox Fruits), stealing from real people (Steal a Brainrot), social roleplay (Brookhaven), player voting (Dress to Impress), asymmetric PvP (Forsaken, MM2), and live "admin abuse" sessions.
6. **Monetization translates mostly to rewarded ads:** Roblox's *developer products* (instant, repeatable: a luck potion, a restock now, a revive, skipping a timer) map onto rewarded placements. *Game passes* (×2 money forever, VIP) map onto permanent unlocks earned through play. Roblox itself now runs rewarded video (6–30 s, opt-in, for experiences with 100K+ DAU).
7. **CrazyGames already has the ports, 1–3 months after each Roblox hit:** Steal Brainrot Online (Aug 2025), Escape Tsunami Brainrot (Mar 2026), Pets Roll: Idle Clicker (Jul 2026: 187 pets, 11 rarities), 99 Nights in the Forest Online, Growden.io (*Grow a Garden*). A port is "close to existing titles" on arrival. **Borrow the systems, not the game.**
8. **Rules we must mind:** PEGI 16 by default for **paid** random items (from June 2026), simulated gambling PEGI 18. CrazyGames requires PEGI 12 and "not misleading". So: odds always shown, no random item for money or for bought currency, a rewarded ad grants a *guaranteed* thing (a tier floor, a second try), never a "spin". No brand names, no "brainrot" characters (taken.ts already warns on steal-base; decisions.md D38: no memes with other people's characters).

### 1.2 Notes per place (what is specific and worth stealing)

- **Grow a Garden.** It contains "almost all" known hooks in one game (a commentator in the Sportskeeda piece). The key one for us is the **5-minute restock that is the same for everyone**. It is an appointment, but a short one: something is always about to change. Roblox's own monetization guide warns that "appointment mechanics and timers" frustrate Roblox users. The restock works because it is an *opportunity* (a rare seed may appear) and never a *wait* (growing can be skipped, nothing blocks play). The **weather** is the second engine: the server's weather decides which mutations can drop, and admins can trigger special weather. Mutations multiply value (Disco ×125), and that turns a crop into a lottery ticket you *grow*. Offline growth closes the loop: "plant, grow, collect pets, return tomorrow" (RoWatcher).
- **Steal a Brainrot / Steal an Egg / Escape Tsunami.** The **conveyor** is a good UI idea: a moving shelf of offers with rarity glows and prices, gone if you hesitate. It is a restock with visible motion. **Income per second** at your base and the **lock timer** create tension. Theft is the multiplayer part. Each peak was an event (Extinct Event, Yin Yang, a concert, a crossover), and "each peak fell off harder than the one before" (RoWatcher).
- **99 Nights / Dead Rails.** A *long run with one number of progress* (night N/99, km N/80), a day/night rhythm (day = gather, night = defend), a **campfire or firebox that must be fed** (one resource that is also your safety), classes as meta. Dead Rails is the better template for us: it works solo, a run ends at a boss, and the reward is stars + bonds.
- **Fisch / Fish It!** The tension-bar minigame is a skill moment of a few seconds that repeats hundreds of times. Rarity and mutations are shaped by **place × bait × rod × weather × event**. The bestiary is the long goal. Fish It! won by *simplifying* Fisch's fishing ("highly accessible").
- **Blox Fruits.** The **4-hour stock rotation**, the same everywhere, plus hourly spawns give players reasons to log in at given times. Hype comes from long-announced reworks.
- **Pet Sim 99 / Adopt Me!** **Fusion** (4 → Neon → Mega Neon) turns duplicates into progress. **Huge** pets are the jackpot tier. Ageing by care tasks turns time into progress you *do*. Paid exclusive eggs are exactly what PEGI now rates 16+.
- **Dress to Impress.** A themed round judged by others. A single-player version needs judges, which means NPCs with tastes. CrazyGames already has **Fashion Battle** and **Fashion Famous** (theme + judges), so the format is taken.
- **DOORS.** **One entity, one cue, one counter**, learnt by dying. A **30-s pre-run shop** for soft currency. **Modifiers** after the first clear (the cheapest content multiplier, like Balatro's stakes in our game-design.md). Revives sold for Robux become a rewarded revive here. The 2026 breakout **Animal Hospital (Anomaly)** (358K CCU, #1 "recommended by Roblox", Aug 2026) shows that "anomaly" night-shift horror is the current wave.
- **Sol's RNG.** The extreme case: no gameplay but the roll. What holds it up is *biomes* (weather making rare auras possible) and the *1-in-X display*. Our Pets Roll neighbour on CrazyGames shows the portal has it too.
- **+1 Speed obbies.** The +1 per step is the fastest visible growth on Roblox. It is a hypercasual loop with prestige (rebirth). CrazyGames already has the obby wave (market-depth-2026-09.md §1: 8+ obby/simulator clones in Mar–Aug 2026).
- **Bee Swarm Simulator.** It stays because systems interact (bee roles × field colours × gear) and players research outside the game. Quests from NPCs ("bear quests") tell you where to go next. That is our `dailyOrder` and hints, in a quest-giver's voice.

## 2. The patterns behind Roblox retention, and the blocks they would need

Each pattern: what it is, the Roblox evidence, what we already have, and a block proposal (name, API sketch, settings).
"Near" = the closest thing in `packages/core/src` today.

### P1. A world clock: restocks, rotations and world events on a shared schedule
- **Evidence:** the Grow a Garden seed shop every 5 min, the same for the whole server, plus a pet-egg shop; the Blox Fruits dealer every 4 h, the same everywhere; weather events; admin events at fixed times ("always at the same times of day", every ~45 days in one game [fan]).
- **Near:** `daySeed`/`dayKey` (a day), `weeklyEvent` (a week, a rule), `seasonPass` (dates), `dailyOrder`. Nothing finer than a day, no "next restock in 3:12", no schedule of events within a day.
- **Block: `worldClock` + `restockShop`** (`@gp/core/clock`, pure, injected `now`):
  ```ts
  const clock = worldClock({ salt: 'storm-geodes', utc: true });              // every player on the same UTC slot sees the same thing
  const shop = restockShop(save, { clock, every: '5m', slots: 6, pool: SEEDS,  // pool: { id, weight, rarity, price }
    persist: () => host.save(save) });
  shop.state();      // { items:[{id, left, price, rarity}], nextIn: 192 /*s*/, slot: 'd2026-10-19#137' }
  shop.buy('frost-quartz'); shop.restockNow();   // restockNow: a rewarded offer, at most once per `restockCooldown`
  const sky = clock.events(SKY_EVENTS);          // [{ id: 'aurora', every: '2h', lasts: '10m', offset: '45m' }] → active + upcoming
  ```
  Settings: `every` (≥ 1 min), `slots`, `pool` weights with a guaranteed-rarity floor per N restocks (a pity), `stockPerItem`, `restockCooldown`, `local: false` (UTC, shared) or `true`. While the game is closed, slots pass without being stored (only what was bought is saved). A clock set back never re-buys. Events have a fixed `every`/`lasts`/`offset`, or a seeded `chance` per slot. **QA hook:** `?clock=+300` jumps time in dev, so `gp qa` can screenshot a restock and an event.
- **Rules:** never "come back in 5 minutes or lose it" framing (D56). The UI shows what is coming, not what was missed. Restocks should land at natural breaks so the midgame ad has a place.

### P2. Variance you can see: rarity tables, "1 in X", pity, luck
- **Evidence:** eggs (Pet Sim 99, Adopt Me!, Grow a Garden), auras (Sol's RNG: 1 in 2 … 1 in 3B), Huge pets, knife boxes (MM2), the rarity grades of Pets Roll on CrazyGames (11 rarities, a bigger reveal per grade).
- **Near:** `seededRng` / `host.rng`, `RunChoices` (pick 1 of 3, reroll). No loot table, no odds display, no pity.
- **Block: `LootTable`** (`@gp/core/loot`):
  ```ts
  const eggs = new LootTable(save, 'eggs', { tiers: RARITIES, rng: host.rng, pity: { tier: 'epic', within: 40 },
    persist: () => host.save(save) });
  const r = eggs.roll('meadow-egg');        // { id, tier, chance: 1/250, pityLeft: 12, first: true }
  eggs.odds('meadow-egg');                  // for the odds panel: [{ tier, chance }] — always shown before a roll
  eggs.roll('meadow-egg', { floor: 'rare' }); // a rewarded "lucky incubator": a guaranteed floor, not an extra spin
  ```
  Settings: tiers with weights, per-source tables, pity, luck multipliers that only *raise the floor or the weights*,
  and a `reveal` helper in `@gp/art/fx` (a glow → shake → burst that scales with the tier, like Pets Roll's "soft
  glow … fireworks").
- **Rules (the validator should enforce them):** odds visible for every roll (a `W_LOOT_ODDS` if `odds()` is never rendered); **no random item for real money or for currency bought with real money** (`E_PAID_RANDOM`: PEGI 16 from June 2026); a rewarded ad buys a *guaranteed* outcome (a floor, a second item, a reroll of a run choice), never "watch an ad to spin". No casino look: no slot reels, no roulette (PEGI 18 for simulated gambling).

### P3. Mutations and variants: one item, many values
- **Evidence:** Grow a Garden (weather mutations, Disco ×125), Fisch (mutations by place, bait, weather, events; Mutation Surge +15 %; an appraiser), Adopt Me! Neon/Mega Neon, MM2 Chroma, Huge pets, Sol's RNG biome auras.
- **Near:** `Tiers` (tiers of a thing), `Roster` (owning). Nothing models "a base × a stack of variants with multipliers and conditions".
- **Block: `Variants`** (`@gp/core/variants`):
  ```ts
  const MUT = variants({ list: [
    { id: 'wet', mult: 2, when: (ctx) => ctx.weather === 'rain', chance: 0.1 },
    { id: 'shocked', mult: 50, when: (ctx) => ctx.weather === 'storm', chance: 0.01 },
    { id: 'frozen-shock', mult: 120, combo: ['frozen', 'shocked'] } ], stack: 'multiply', max: 3 });
  const item = MUT.apply({ base: 'crystal', variants: [] }, { weather: 'storm', rng });
  MUT.value(item, baseValue);               // the multiplied value; MUT.label(item) → "Frozen Shocked Crystal"
  ```
  Settings: `stack` (multiply / add / highest), `max`, `combo` (a recipe of variants → a named one), `when` (the conditions of the world: the clock's weather, a place, a tool). It also feeds the index (P4) and the balance model (`balanceModel()` has to see the expected value of a harvest).

### P4. The collection index: a visible set to complete
- **Evidence:** Pet Sim 99 index, Fisch bestiary, Sol's RNG aura list, DOORS entities, Adopt Me! Neon versions. Pets Roll on CrazyGames pays coins and buff charges for a finished collection. Endowed-progress and album effects are in market-depth-2026-09.md #17.
- **Near:** `Roster` (an owned list), `Tiers`; the merge genre lists "collection" as a must-have with only `Tiers`/`Roster` as blocks.
- **Block: `Collection` + `mountIndex`** (`@gp/core/collection`):
  ```ts
  const index = new Collection(save, 'index', { entries: SPECIES, variants: ['normal', 'glow', 'radiant'],
    milestones: [{ at: 10, reward: { mult: 1.05 } }, { at: 'set:forest', reward: { egg: 'forest-plus' } }],
    persist: () => host.save(save) });
  index.record('moss-fox', 'glow');         // → { new: true, milestone: {...} | null, seen: 23, total: 90 }
  mountIndex(ui, index, { host, view: (e, v) => ({ icon: speciesIcon(e, v), label: host.t(`sp.${e}`) }),
    hidden: 'silhouette' });                // pages, never a scrolling list (same rules as mountSeasonTrack)
  ```
  Settings: entries × variants, `seen` vs `owned`, sets (a region or a family), milestones (permanent multipliers, unlocks), the silhouette of an unknown entry, a "new" badge. QA: one screen at every size, a page at a time (the season track's rules).

### P5. Income that keeps going: income per second, offline growth, rebirth
- **Evidence:** Steal a Brainrot (income per second at the base, rebirth), Grow a Garden (offline growth), Pet Sim 99 (rebirths gate Huge chances), +1 Speed obbies (rebirth multipliers), Escape Tsunami Brainrot on CrazyGames ("the empire keeps generating income even while you are away").
- **Near: we have this.** `offlineEarnings`, `Prestige`, `Sites`/`mountSitesMap`, `UpgradeShop`, `welcomeBack`. **But** they are idle-only (`OFFLINE_GENRES = ['idle']`, `PRESTIGE_GENRES = ['idle']`; `W_GENRE_BLOCK` warns elsewhere). The Roblox hits are hybrids: a tycoon with action raids, a speed obby with rebirth, a run game with a growing camp.
- **Change (not a new block):** allow `Prestige` for `hypercasual`/`platformer` ("rebirth": a simulator obby) and `offlineEarnings` for a declared hybrid (`money.genre: tycoon` + an action sub-loop) instead of warning. The rule should be "an idle *layer* exists in game.yaml", not "the genre is idle".

### P6. Time-boxed live moments: admin abuse → a festival hour
- **Evidence:** the Grow a Garden × Steal a Brainrot "admin war" (2025-08-23: Roblox's record of 47.4M concurrent). Events of 15–35 min at announced times. Weekly updates as the floor (Steal a Brainrot Update 44; "games that stop updating decline within weeks", RoWatcher). The criticism: "only in the short term" (Beebom).
- **Near:** `weeklyEvent` (a weekly rule, a goal), `seasonPass`. No event *hours*.
- **Block:** part of `worldClock` (P1): `clock.events([{ id: 'festival', weekly: 'SAT 18:00', lasts: '30m', rule: 'double-mutations' }])`, plus a title banner "Festival in 2 h 14 min — mutations ×2 for everyone". It is honest because every player gets it at that time. **Not** a fake "admin is online". An update cadence (content every 2–4 weeks) is a process, not a block: `gp submission` already has the update path.

### P7. Rule-learning threats: one entity, one cue, one counter
- **Evidence:** DOORS (each entity's cue and counter, learnt by dying; modifiers after a clear), Forsaken (killer kits), Animal Hospital (Anomaly), the Exit 8 lineage.
- **Near:** `hints()` (one hint at a time), `mountResults()` (the next goal), `RunChoices`. No bestiary of threats, no "you died to X: do Y" card.
- **Block: `threatBook`** (small): `book.met('rush')`, `book.died('rush')` → the results line "Rush: hide when the lights flicker" with a drawn icon; the book becomes the index (P4) of entities; `modifiers` unlock after the first clear (the run tiers that game-design.md already recommends).

### P8. Codes and social calls to action
- **Evidence:** every Roblox hit posts codes (free gems, potions) at like milestones, after updates, as apologies; they expire quickly.
- **Near:** nothing. **Value on CrazyGames: low.** There is no like goal, and the audience doesn't follow a developer's Discord. Codes are only worth it for a game with its own community (the owner's site, YouTube Playables). If we ever do it: `redeemCode(save, input, { codes: HASHED, until })`, with hashed codes in the build and an expiry date. Rank: not in the top 5.

### P9. Async social without a server
- **Evidence:** visiting other players' gardens and gifts (Grow a Garden), showing auras (Sol's RNG), knife values (MM2). The social flex is half of Roblox (Naavik: a culture "built around social flex").
- **Near:** `copySeed` / `seedField` / `runSeedOf` (a run seed you copy, share and type back): the pattern exists for *runs*.
- **Block: `shareCode`** (`@gp/core/share`): encode a compact, signed (not secret) snapshot of a *showcase* (a den, a garden, a lineup of 5 pets, a best run's ghost) into a short string or URL hash; decode it into a read-only "visit", a ghost race, or a raid level. That is real async multiplayer without a server, made by players, the way Clash attacks a snapshot of a base. See §3.

### P10. Social proof and status (what we should *not* fake)
Roblox shows other players' auras, Huge pets and big gardens in the same space. On the web the honest equivalents
are: the portal's own weekly leaderboard (invited games), a share card, the share code (P9), a "first discovered on
<date>" stamp in the index, and the rarity reveal itself. See §3 for the line we don't cross.

## 3. "Fake multiplayer" for single-player web: what is honest and allowed

**What CrazyGames says (from our requirements note and its docs):** the game "is clear about what it is. It isn't
misleading". "Playing alone is as prominent as playing with friends if both are offered"; "If playing alone is not
available, the game clearly explains that." PEGI 12. Leaderboards are a portal feature for **invited games** only:
one leaderboard per game, weekly seasons from Monday to Monday at 09:00 UTC, trophies for top 1/2/3 and top 1/5/10 %,
an encrypted client score or the server API, and a "Leaderboard Guide" text of ≤ 50 characters. A separate
`user.addScore` exists for the Game Challenge feature. **The docs we found do not mention bots.** .io games on the portal
commonly fill rounds with bots. Our genres.md already says .io players "come for other players (or bots that pass for
them)". The line below is the platform's own, stricter than anything written by the portal.

| Technique | What it is | Honest? | Rule for our games |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shared world by clock seed** | every player sees the same restock, weather and festival at the same UTC time (P1, P6) | **yes:** it is true | may say "the same for every player"; never print a fake count of players in it |
| **Real sky / calendar events** | real dates computed in code (full moons, the Perseids on Aug 12–13, the Geminids on Dec 13–14, solstices) | yes | a good source of free, honest, recurring events |
| **Ghosts of your own runs** | replay your best run as a translucent ghost (a race, a daily course) | yes | label it "your best" |
| **Developer ghosts / par** | recorded runs or par scores shipped in the build | yes, if labelled | "par set by the developer", "medal: gold 12,000"; never a player name |
| **Named NPC rivals** | characters with portraits and personalities who raid, trade or compete ("Maple's den") | yes: they are characters | they are drawn as characters, not as usernames; no fake chat, no fake friend requests |
| **Bots in a round (.io)** | bots fill an arena | grey | allowed as opponents only. No "X players online" counter, no human-looking chat, a solo/offline label on the menu. The safe version names them as the characters they are |
| **Async trading with NPC traders** | a market of traders with wants, value ranges and a daily rotation | yes | they are shopkeepers. No claim that a human is on the other side; never real-money value |
| **Share codes / visits / raids on a snapshot** | a player exports their den or lineup as a code; a friend imports it, visits or raids a copy (P9) | **yes: real players, async** | the owner loses nothing (it is a copy); show where the code came from; no free text inside codes (no chat to moderate) |
| **The portal's weekly leaderboard** | CrazyGames Leaderboards (invited games), Game Challenge `addScore` | yes, real | the score must be one honest number (seeded runs: the daily, not custom seeds — seed.ts already says so) |
| **A local leaderboard with NPC entries** | "Top keepers this week" with invented names presented as people | **no** | never: it is misleading |
| **"12,483 players online" / "Ana just found a Huge!" toasts** | fabricated social proof | **no** | never |

**Recommendation:** a small `rivals` helper (named NPC characters with a schedule and a behaviour seed) plus `shareCode`
(P9) cover most of the fun of Steal-a-X and garden visits without one dishonest pixel. Real leaderboards wait for the
portal's services (the D54 request). Add the portal's Leaderboards and Game Challenge to that request: they are the
portal's own retention feature (market-depth-2026-09.md #5) and they need only the SDK, not our server.

### 4.1 Night Shift Rules — roguelite, the anomaly wave in 2D
- **One sentence:** work a night shift through 50 rooms of a strange roadside motel; every guest that isn't a guest has one tell and one rule, and you learn them by surviving.
- **Inspired by:** DOORS (entities with a cue and a counter, knobs and a 30-s pre-run shop, revives, modifiers after a clear), Animal Hospital (Anomaly) (2026's night-shift breakout), the Exit 8 lineage.
- **Closest on CrazyGames:** [Doors Castle](https://www.crazygames.nl/spel/doors-castle) (keys, doors, monsters), [Horror Room: Scary Hotel Tycoon](https://www.crazygames.com/game/horror-room-scary-hotel-tycoon) (a different genre). No anomaly or Exit 8-like game turned up in portal searches (off-portal: Exit 8, Anomaly Exit, There Exists Nobody on Steam/itch).
- **The twist:** **rules stack.** From room 20 two entities share a room, and their counters conflict (one wants you to hide, one wants you to keep the lamp on you). The player must find the *order*. A rule-combination puzzle under time pressure, not a jump-scare corridor.
- **Money design:** session 14 min. A break every 10 rooms (an elevator, ≈ 2–3 min) where the midgame ad lives. Rewarded: a revive once per session; a "lantern" that shows one entity's tell for the next room, offered on the death screen or in the elevator, never in a room; ×2 tips (soft currency) on the results. Return: a **daily shift seed**, the same for everyone (`dailyRunSeed`), modifiers unlocked after the first clear (run tiers), a threat book (P7/P4) to complete.
- **Blocks:** `RunChoices` (the pre-shift shop), `Roster` (staff: a night porter who hears better, a cleaner who hides faster), `dailyRunSeed`, `hints()`, `mountResults()`; new: `threatBook` (P7), `Collection` (P4).
- **Watch out:** PEGI 12 means spooky, not gory: no blood, no death animations, a "you fainted" card. A quiet start (first-minute.md): the first 3 rooms are safe. Sound carries the tells, so add a visual twin for every audio cue (accessibility, muted tabs).

### 4.2 Hatch Lab — merge, rarity you engineer instead of roll
- **One sentence:** hatch creature eggs with shown odds, raise them through care tasks, then fuse and cross-breed them by a visible genetics rule to make the rare ones yourself.
- **Inspired by:** Adopt Me! (ageing by tasks; 4 Full Grown → Neon → Mega Neon), Pet Simulator 99 (eggs, Huge tier, the index, zones), Sol's RNG (the 1-in-X reveal).
- **Closest on CrazyGames:** [Pets Roll: Idle Clicker](https://www.crazygames.com/game/pets-roll-idle-clicker) (pure rolls, Jul 2026), [Rare Pets](https://www.crazygames.com/fr/game/rare-pets-fki) (merge café, Apr 2026); off-portal Merge Zoo (mobile), Mon-Mons (itch: breed to a target).
- **The twist:** **the rarest creatures can't be rolled, only bred.** Pattern, colour and size follow a simple visible inheritance rule (two parents → a predictable spread). The index shows targets as silhouettes with hints ("striped like its mother, glowing like neither"). Rolls start the collection, breeding finishes it. That puts it apart from Pets Roll's luck and keeps the random part small and honest.
- **Money design:** session 15 min, a break at every egg reveal or fusion (≈ every 2 min). Rewarded: "lucky incubator" (the next hatch has a guaranteed rare floor), offline ×2 (the creatures' zone income while away), a second nest for 10 minutes. Return: a daily "wanted creature" (`dailyOrder`), a weekly species event (`weeklyEvent`), the index.
- **Blocks:** `Sites`/`mountSitesMap` (zones), `offlineEarnings`, `Prestige` ("a new lab"), `dailyOrder`, `weeklyEvent`; new: `LootTable` (P2), `Variants` (P3), `Collection` (P4).
- **Watch out:** no paid eggs, ever (PEGI 16). Odds on every egg. Creatures of our own design (no Adopt Me! or Pet Sim look-alikes, no "Huge" naming).

### 4.3 Last Lamp — roguelite, a riverboat journey through fog
- **One sentence:** steer a small riverboat 30 river-miles through a foggy night country, where the one fuel you burn is your speed, your light and your warmth.
- **Inspired by:** Dead Rails (80 km, feed the firebox, night is when runs end, a boss, bonds for classes), 99 Nights in the Forest (survive nights, a camp that grows within the run).
- **Closest on CrazyGames:** [99 Nights in the Forest Online](https://www.crazygames.com/game/99-nights-online), [99 Nights bloxd.io](https://www.crazygames.nl/spel/99-nights-bloxd-io), [Noob vs Cops](https://www.crazygames.com/game/noob-vs-cops) (boat, zombies), [Cavern: From the Fog](https://crazygames.com/game/cavern-from-the-fog) (a lantern, TD + mining). No boat-journey survival roguelite found.
- **The twist:** **one fuel, three uses.** Burning hard shortens the night but dims the lamp the fog creatures fear. Running dark is fast and dangerous. Every night is a budget decision you see on the lamp itself. By day you dock at one of 3 piers (`RunChoices`: scavenge, hire a deckhand, patch the hull).
- **Money design:** session 14 min, a run of 10–14 min. A break at every dawn (a night ≈ 90 s, a day ≈ 30 s, so a break every ≈ 2 min). Rewarded: a revive once per session (the boat limps to the next pier), ×2 river tokens on the results, a boat or captain trial for one run. Return: **a daily river** (a daily seed, the forecast's top lever), boats and captains (`Roster`), a weekly river rule (`weeklyEvent`: "low water", "heavy fog").
- **Blocks:** `Roster`, `RunChoices`, `dailyRunSeed`, `weeklyEvent`, `seasonPass`, `saveRun`; possibly a small `dayNight` phase clock.
- **Watch out:** our survivors warning (game-ideas.md): don't let the night become an auto-attack swarm. The player *aims* the lamp's beam (the core input), and creatures retreat from light, they don't die to auto-fire.

### 4.4 Storm Geodes — idle, mutations by weather *sequence*
- **One sentence:** grow crystals in a cave while you call the cave's weather (steam, frost, lightning, aurora); a crystal's mutation is the order of weathers it lived through.
- **Inspired by:** Grow a Garden (a 5-min restock, weather mutations, offline growth), Fisch (mutations shaped by conditions, the Mutation Surge event).
- **Closest on CrazyGames:** [Crystalia Idle Clicker](https://www.crazygames.com/it/game/crystalia-idle-clicker) (crystal mining idle, Apr 2026), [Idle Hurricane](https://www.crazygames.com/game/idle-hurricane) (grow a storm), [Garden Idle](https://crazygames.com/game/garden-idle) and Growden.io (garden). This is **not** a garden or a weather puzzle: no `cozy-idle` theme words (garden, aquarium, fish) and no steering clouds over fields (`weather-puzzle`, our Nimbus Drift).
- **The twist:** **the player is the weather, and a mutation is a recipe.** Frost then lightning makes "Shattered" (×20). Steam, frost, steam makes "Opal" (×40). The weather charge is limited, so you plan sequences across crystals at different growth stages. A 48-entry index of recipes is found by experiment. The shared "aurora hour" (world clock, same for everyone) is the only time the top tier can form.
- **Money design:** session 15 min, a break at each crack-and-sell (≈ every 2–3 min, which also fixes the idle card's "breaks" lever). Rewarded: a free weather front (a charge refill), offline ×2, a "restock now" for the seed-crystal stall (once per 15 min). Return: offline growth, the shared aurora schedule, a daily order, prestige to a new cave.
- **Blocks:** `UpgradeShop`, `offlineEarnings`, `Prestige`, `Sites`, `welcomeBack`, `dailyOrder`; new: `worldClock`/`restockShop` (P1), `Variants` (P3), `Collection` (P4).

### 4.5 Comet Angler — incremental, fishing the night sky
- **One sentence:** cast a line into the night sky and reel in comets and constellation creatures with a tension minigame, filling a star atlas whose rarest entries appear only during real meteor showers.
- **Inspired by:** Fisch and Fish It! (a tension bar, rods and baits, mutations by weather and event, the bestiary), Sol's RNG (biomes that make rare things possible).
- **Closest on CrazyGames:** [Fish Catch Idle](https://www.crazygames.com/game/fish-catch-idle), [Underwater Hunting](https://www.crazygames.com/de/game/underwater-hunting), Tiny Fishing; off-portal Underneath the Stars (itch: fish upward for celestial creatures), Space Fishing (Epic). That last pair is the risk: the theme exists off-portal (uniqueness ≈ 75).
- **The twist:** **the sky is a real calendar.** Moon phases, the Perseids (Aug 12–13), the Geminids (Dec 13–14) and solstices are computed in code, so every player has the same sky, honestly. A shooting-star hour each evening (world clock) is when comets bite. Constellation creatures must be *linked*: catching three stars of one shape in order completes a creature (a light puzzle inside the reel).
- **Money design:** session 15 min, a break after each "night" (≈ 3 min, the sky turns to dawn). Rewarded: ×2 stardust on a catch, a lucky lure (a rare floor for the next 3 casts), a second reel when the line snaps (a "continue"). Return: the real sky calendar, `dailyOrder` ("tonight: a red comet"), the atlas, `seasonPass` by astronomical season.
- **Blocks:** `UpgradeShop`, `dailyOrder`, `seasonPass`; new: `worldClock` (with an astronomy helper), `LootTable`, `Variants`, `Collection`.

### 4.6 Raccoon Rummage — tycoon + action raids (lowest: a taken pair)
- **One sentence:** run a raccoon den whose shelf of junk-treasures earns shine every second, buy from the garbage-truck conveyor, and raid three rival dens in short stealth dashes where everything you carry slows you down.
- **Inspired by:** Steal a Brainrot (conveyor, income per second, theft, a lock, rebirth), Steal an Egg (raids into richer zones), Grow a Garden 2 (nighttime theft).
- **Closest on CrazyGames:** [Steal Brainrot Online](https://www.crazygames.com/es/game/steal-brainrot-online), Merge & Steal Brainrot, Steal Brainrot Survivors, Treasure Champion: Chest Capture. `taken.ts` **steal-base** matches it, so `W_CONCEPT_TAKEN` will warn.
- **The twist (it has to be strong):** the rivals are **named NPC characters with readable routines** (one sleeps at dawn, one sets traps, one counts her shelf). Each raid is a 30-s stealth puzzle against that routine, not a race. **Friends' dens arrive as share codes** (P9): a real friend's den becomes a raid level, and they lose nothing (it is a copy).
- **Money design:** session 15 min, a break at each "garbage day" restock (every 3 min). Rewarded: ×2 shine for 10 min, a lock extension, a second try at a failed raid (a revive). Return: offline shine, prestige ("a bigger tree"), a weekly rival.
- **Verdict:** keep it as the reference design for `rivals` + `shareCode`, not as the next game. It sits in a taken pair, under 10 brainrot ports.

**Considered and dropped:** a Dress-to-Impress-like contest (Fashion Battle and Fashion Famous already have theme + judges; voting needs people) · a Sol's-RNG-like pure roller (Pets Roll is live, and it sits closest to PEGI's gambling lines) · a +1-speed obby (the portal's obby wave, 3D) · a 99 Nights clone (live on the portal) · a Bee Swarm-like (our own swarm games: Petri Panic, Germ Bait).
