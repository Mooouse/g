# Game trends 2025–2026 for simple 2D HTML5 games (research, 2026-09-30)

> Trend research for [game-ideas.md](../guides/game-ideas.md). The proxy blocked the full reports (AppMagic, Sensor Tower,
> GameDev Reports, Medium, HowToMarketAGame), so numbers come from search snippets. Secondary sources, press releases and

## Market context
- **Mobile 2025:** game IAP ≈ $81.75B (+1.3%), downloads −7.2% to 50.4B — growth comes from spending, not installs ([Sensor Tower via Cinevva, 2026](https://app.cinevva.com/guides/casual-games-trends-2026) *(secondary)*; [9to5Mac, 2026-01-21](https://9to5mac.com/2026/01/21/sensor-towers-state-of-mobile-2026-tiktok-dominates-ai-apps-surge-games-lose-ground/)).
- **Puzzle is the growth genre:** $14.4B IAP in 2025 ([Sensor Tower](https://sensortower.com/press/press-release-sensor-tower-state-of-gaming-gaming-drove-52-billion-downloads-82b-iap-revenue-on-mobile-and-12b-premium-revenue-on-steam)); H1 2026 $8.07B, ≈ +20% YoY while downloads fell ≈ 12% ([GameDev Reports](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/sensor-tower-the-gaming-market-in) *(snippet)*).
- **Hybrid-casual** (simple core + progression + IAP) was the only segment whose IAP grew in 2025: +20% to $4.2B ([Game World Observer, 2026-01-21](https://gameworldobserver.com/2026/01/21/in-2025-the-revenue-of-the-mobile-gaming-market-grew-but-only-slightly-analysis)).
- **Web portals:** CrazyGames' study of 1.74B US sessions (May 2025–May 2026): hypercasual #1 by share of hours (79%), puzzle 21%; average session 30 min; weekdays +22% over weekends; peak 1 pm ET ([PocketGamer.biz, 2026](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/report-hypercasual-and-puzzle-games-dominate-the-browser-gaming-space/)). Yandex Games: 80% of players over 25, 58% women; bubble shooter and match-3 lead ([WN Hub, ~Jan 2026](https://wnhub.io/news/other/item-49945)).

## Why the top trends work
- **Sort / block / arrow / colour-jam:** the whole puzzle is visible; one touch turns chaos into order; short levels with a near-miss. Money comes from a "fail offer" (continue for a booster), not difficulty spikes ([AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/fail-offers)); in H1'25 sort downloads grew +14% while revenue grew 2.2× ([AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/research/casual-report-h1-2025)).
- **Daily puzzles:** one a day (scarcity), streaks, spoiler-free share results, 3–5 min — retention with almost no content budget.
- **Idle:** numbers always go up, progress while away, runs beside other things; fits 30-minute web sessions.
- **Survivors / BALL x PIT:** one input with a strong upgrade choice every 30–60 s.
- **Kids:** parents pay for ad-free, safe, offline, open-ended play; the child needs no reading, no failure, instant feedback.

## Fading trends
- Telegram tap-to-earn (Hamster Kombat lost > 96% of players — [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/news/nfts-and-web3/hamster-kombat-loses-259-million-players-amid-86-drop-blockchain-gaming-01e15538)).
- Pure ad-only hypercasual on **mobile** (UA costs) — still king on web portals ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/what-happened-to-hypercasual-the-markets-evolution-over-the-past-year/)).
- Classic match-3: −0.4% YoY, "intensely saturated" ([GameRefinery, Dec 2025](https://www.gamerefinery.com/mobile-game-market-review-december-2025/)).
- Meme waves peak within weeks (analyst's judgement).

## Legal and originality risks
- **Trade dress:** in 2024 the NYT sent DMCA notices to ≈ 1,900 forks of a Wordle clone over the 5×6 grid, green/yellow tiles and keyboard ([Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/nyt-dmca-takedown-request-wordle-clones-reactle-1851320538)). Own grid, colours, name, share format.
- **Meme IP is contested** (Tung Tung Tung Sahur lawsuits — [NPR, 2026-08-19](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/19/nx-s1-5867638/artificial-intelligence-brainrot-memes-copyright-spyder-tung-tung-sahur)). Never use named meme characters.
- **Gambling looks:** Balatro was first rated PEGI 18 over poker hands ([Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/balatro-s-contentious-pegi-18-rating-has-been-amended-thanks-to-mitigating-fantastical-elements-)). No slots/poker for kids or Google Play casual.
- **Google Play:** Repetitive Content risk is highest in exactly these trends (thousands of near-identical sort/block games). Families is tightening: certified ad networks, Age Signals API in 2026 ([Android Developers Blog, Jul 2026](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/07/google-play-age-signals-api-safer-experiences.html)).
- **Brand names:** never "Block Blast", "Wordle", "Screw Jam" etc. in titles or store texts.
